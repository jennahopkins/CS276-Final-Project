### Project Title & Description:
The game Murder Mystery is a puzzle-adventure genre inspired by the social game ‘Mafia’. Players are a detective driving in a car around crime-scene neighborhoods trying to find and pick up clues to solve a murder. Collect enough evidence and get to the murderer’s house before the timer runs out to successfully arrest the murderer and restore justice to the neighborhood. Levels start off simple, but as players get more skilled at their detective skills the murders will become harder and harder to solve, separating the extraordinary detectives from the rest.

### Features Completed:
- Full Levels 1 and 2 completed
- Forward/backwards and steering of the car through WASD and arrow keys
- Start/end level UI popups
- Click on clues for a UI popup and add to inventory
- Click on people for a UI popup
- Win/loss logic- Player wins the level if they have specific clues and are next to the murderer at the end of the timer
- Unlock next level when previous one is won
- Ability to restart level or move to next one after level is played
- Pause menu that can bring player back to main menu
- Main menu screen with all levels
- Only unlocked levels can be clicked on from main menu and played

- How to play button and UI popup from main menu

- Timer UI

### How To Play:
Use the W-S or up-down arrow keys to move forwards and backwards, and the A-D or left-right arrow keys to steer. Click on people in the neighborhood to talk to them and try to figure out what happened. Click on potential clues to the murder to learn more about them and pick them up. By the time the timer runs out, you need to have picked up the right clues and have brought them to the murderer to solve the case and restore justice to the neighborhood!
Click the pause button at the top center of the screen to pause the level and have the chance to return to the main menu or resume the level. At the main menu, click on any of the unlocked levels to play them. Unlock levels by solving the murder in the previous one!
